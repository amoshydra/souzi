Privacy Policy for Souzi (搜字)

Souzi is an offline-first dictionary. It does not collect, transmit, or share personal data.

Data stored on your device

Search history, favorites, and installed dictionaries are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB) or in the app's private storage on Android. They never leave your device.

Network use

Souzi makes network requests only when you explicitly download an optional dictionary pack (the full English dictionary) or the Chinese dictionary from its official source. These downloads go directly from the source to your device.

Third parties

Souzi does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It does not share data with third parties.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/amoshydra/souzi.
